The final extraction phase in which, after all scopes complete, a single agent reads their outputs and produces the Level 4 semantic layer for the whole codebase — concepts spanning scopes, patterns replicated across modules, cross-scope semantic edges, and the unified tag and cluster structure. It is the step that makes scoped extraction cohere as a whole rather than a collection of siloed analyses.
