term: extraction-scope
definition: A logically coherent subset of a codebase that can be analyzed as a unit during insight extraction, identified by Claude during the planning phase. Scopes may nest and may reference shared sub-scopes; each root scope runs as a parallel sub-agent, and a scope's granularity is judged by cohesion and shared-use rather than a fixed file count.
