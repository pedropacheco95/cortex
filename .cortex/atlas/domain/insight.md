term: insight
definition: The load-bearing .cortex/ module that holds Cortex's persistent, queryable, actively-refreshed understanding of a codebase — rich per-file entries, concept graph, and cluster assignments produced by LLM extraction. It is ungated (inferred context rather than authority — where it conflicts with a compass rule or a spec, the gated layer wins) and it absorbs v1's anatomy module.
