# Constellation — Overview

## What this is

Visible proof the project is understood. The outcome is that anyone — a collaborator, a stakeholder, a future maintainer — can open a navigable map and see that the project is understood in a structured, traceable way that isn't trapped in one person's head.

## What it covers

**Outcomes written:**

- **A stakeholder sees, at a glance, that the project is understood** — one connected, traceable map of code, rules, memory, and promises, with gaps shown honestly.

- **A stakeholder opens the project map and finds their way around it** — one local command, a private read-only viewer, five honest lenses including "what's unconnected" and "just this area".

_Planned outcomes (not yet written):_

- **A navigable map anyone can open** — understanding is something you can look at, not something you have to ask for.
- **Proof, not just claims** — the structure visibly shows the project is understood.
- **Traceable connections** — a reader can follow how pieces relate rather than taking it on faith.
- **Knowledge out of one head** — understanding lives in the open, surviving any single person leaving.

## Why it's grouped this way

This group exists because understanding that lives only in one person's head is fragile and unprovable. A navigable, traceable view turns that understanding into something a collaborator or stakeholder can see and trust for themselves. Making comprehension visible and shareable is the outcome that belongs here.

What deliberately does not belong here is the production or upkeep of the underlying knowledge — the map of the code, the rules, the memory all live in their own groups. This group is about presenting that knowledge as visible proof, not generating it.
